Sleep-stage classifier: work done

Project folder D:\Ai agent · 11 October 2026

A neural network, trained from scratch on the Sleep-EDF Expanded dataset, that labels every 30 seconds of an overnight recording as one of five sleep stages. On 20 recordings from people it never saw in training, it agrees with the expert scoring on 82.4% of epochs.

1. Dataset and preprocessing

The raw data is 197 overnight recordings (153 from the cassette study, 44 from the telemetry study), 8.1 GB of EDF files, each with an expert-scored hypnogram. sleep_staging/preprocess.py does the following to every recording:

StepWhat it does
ChannelsKeeps EEG Fpz-Cz, EEG Pz-Oz and horizontal EOG, all at 100 Hz
FilterBand-pass 0.3–35 Hz
Epochs and labels30-second epochs labelled from the hypnogram; stages 3 and 4 merged into N3
TrimSleep period plus 30 minutes of wake on either side
Drop1,988 epochs scored as movement or unknown
NormaliseEach channel standardised within the recording, clipped at ±20 standard deviations

The output is one file per recording in data/sleep_edf/ (4 GB in total) and an index.csv summary.

3. The neural network

PartDetail
Input3 channels × 3,000 samples per epoch, 20 consecutive epochs at a time
Epoch encoder1D CNN: a wide first filter (half a second), then three narrower layers
Sequence modelBidirectional GRU, so each epoch is scored using its neighbours
Size2,012,165 parameters
SplitBy subject: 157 training, 20 validation and 20 test recordings
ImbalanceRare stages weighted more in the loss
TrainingAdamW, cosine learning rate; about 9 minutes on a GPU
StoppingStopped early at epoch 17; the saved model is from epoch 11, the best on validation

5. Classical baseline

sleep_staging/features.py computes 21 hand-made features per epoch (delta, theta, alpha and beta band power plus the three Hjorth parameters, for each of the three channels). sleep_staging/baseline.py trains logistic regression on them, scored on the same test recordings.

7. Known limits

  • The model was trained once with the first settings chosen; nothing has been tuned.
  • One train/validation/test split was used, not cross-validation, so the scores carry some luck of the split.
  • The 44 telemetry recordings include nights on a sleep drug (temazepam); they are mixed in with the healthy-sleep recordings.
  • There is no script yet to score a new raw recording from outside the dataset.
